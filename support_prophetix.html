App Privacy Policy

Your quick help center for questions, troubleshooting & privacy

1. Introduction

Prophetix is a casual intuition-based game designed for entertainment and mental focus. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Game Data: Your score is updated after each prediction. Your highest score and chosen leaderboard name are stored for rankings.
  • User Name (Optional): If you enter a name, it will be visible on the leaderboard. If you do not, the default display is “Autonomy.”
  • Device Information: Basic technical data (device type, iOS/Android version, crash logs) may be collected to improve performance.
  • No Financial Data: Market charts are simulated for gameplay only. No investment accounts or transactions are collected.

3. How We Use Data

  • To save and display your highest score in the leaderboard.
  • To improve gameplay performance (bug fixing, crash reports).
  • To provide troubleshooting support if you contact us.

4. Third-Party Services

Charts are generated from third-party sources for gameplay simulation only. No personal data is shared with these providers. We do not sell or share player data with advertisers.

5. Your Choices

  • You can update your leaderboard name anytime in the app.
  • You may request deletion of any stored data by contacting support.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. However, no system is 100% secure.

7. Disclaimer

Prophetix is a game only. The simulated stock market charts are not investment advice and should not be used for financial decisions.
