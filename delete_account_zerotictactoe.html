Delete Account - Zero Tic Tac Toe

If you want to delete your Zero Tic Tac Toe account and associated data, you can request deletion using one of the methods below.

  1. Open the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete account.
  2. Or email us at thanhbinh4388@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Zero Tic Tac Toe account".

Please include your Player ID or the login method used in the app so we can identify your account.

Data Deleted

  • Unity Authentication account data connected to Zero Tic Tac Toe
  • Game progress or player data stored by the app, if any
  • Leaderboard or cloud save data, if used

Retention

We usually process deletion requests within 30 days. Some limited information may be kept if required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact us at thanhbinh4388@gmail.com.
